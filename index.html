A space to feel at home

At Saint Helen Cafe we believe in good coffee, fresh food and an atmosphere where you feel right at home. Our rustic brick walls and comfortable seating invite you to enjoy breakfast, brunch or lunch with quality ingredients, vegetarian and vegan options, and always friendly service.

Located in the heart of Hawthorn, every visit is an experience: the aroma of freshly ground coffee, warm light streaming through our windows and the sound of great conversation. Come discover why we're the neighbourhood's favourite corner.
